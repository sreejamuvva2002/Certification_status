Classification Notes

Brake pads (organic, semi-metallic, ceramic, low-metallic NAO), brake shoes, brake discs (rotors, drilled, slotted, blanked), brake callipers, master cylinders, brake lines (pre-assembled), and drum brakes for motor vehicles are all classified under 8708.30. Major brands: Brembo, Akebono, Bosch, TRW, ATE, Ferodo, EBC, Hawk, StopTech, Wagner, Power Stop. The friction material (ceramic vs metallic vs organic) does not affect classification. Aftermarket performance brake upgrades (Brembo GT, StopTech Trophy, AP Racing) share the heading 8708.30. Brake fluid is NOT 8708 — it is 3819 (hydraulic brake fluid). ABS control modules are 8708.30 as part of the braking system. Brake-pedal assemblies are 8708.29 (other body parts). Electronic parking brake actuators are 8708.30 (part of brake system). Motorcycle brake pads and brakes are 8714.10 (parts of motorcycles), NOT 8708.30. Bicycle disc brakes are 8714.94 (bicycle brakes). Semi-finished brake-rotor blanks for further machining are 7326 (iron/steel articles). Brake pad friction material (raw mixed into shape but not mounted to backing plate) is 6813.20 (friction material). Chinese-origin brake parts have been subject to Section 301 List 1-3 additional tariffs — verify current US rate. OEM vs aftermarket distinction does not affect HS classification but may affect emissions/safety approval (DOT, ECE R90 for EU).
