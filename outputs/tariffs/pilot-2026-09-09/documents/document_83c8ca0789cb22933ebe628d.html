Aaron Snyder 1y

It’s a tricky situation. Hopefully vendors are planning ahead, whether they’d landed the product prior and already knew the final tariff amount, or calculated it in advance of the quote and adjusted with any changes. Our goal as a vendor is ”no surprises”. But, with the situation changing rapidly every day, complications with timing and cutoffs, and delays in tariff bills hitting shipping accounts weeks after the product has landed, sometimes surprises happen. In those cases, we rely on constant communication and our strong customer relationships to problemsolve together. Let’s face it, vendors probably arent making 30%+ on resale, so _not_ passing it along isn’t likely an option, but we can all try to be better with planning and communication.
