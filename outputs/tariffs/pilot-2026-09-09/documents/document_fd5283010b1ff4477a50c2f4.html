Parts Development Cost Management Specialist

Date:  Aug 19, 2026
Location: 

Ellabell, GA, US

Job Req ID:  4035

JOB TITLE

Parts Development Cost Management

WHAT YOU WILL DO

This role is part of the Cost Management team at Hyundai Motor Group Metaplant America(HMGMA) and reports to the Sr. Manager of Cost Management, Parts Development. As a Cost Analyst, you will play a crucial role in assessing and optimizing the financial aspects of our vehicle parts and supply chain. Your primary responsibility will be to analyze all aspects of parts cost, monitor changes, and ensure accurate weekly/monthly financial reporting. You will collaborate with cross-functional teams to provide valuable insights that drive cost efficiency and profitability. The role requires the successful candidate to have strong accounting, systems, and analytical skills along with high attention to detail and the ability to work effectively with a variety of stake holders.

Material Cost Analysis
·    Lead vehicle-level material cost calculations for both new and existing vehicles, tracking target cost achievement and reporting progress to upper management.
·    Gather and consolidate material costs for all HMGMA-built vehicles, analyzing and reporting cost variances by vehicle and supplier.
·    Assess cost impacts of price fluctuations due to tariffs, raw materials, labor, and other factors. Collaborate with Commercial and Procurement teams to support pricing decisions.
·    Validate, track, and forecast procurement costs for vehicle parts to ensure accurate financial planning.
·    Analyze changes in product design, raw materials, and manufacturing processes to assess their impact on overall costs.
·    Evaluate cost effects on profitability, providing insights to optimize cost efficiency and financial performance.
 
Investment Budget Management
·    Oversee the overall tooling budget, ensuring alignment with new vehicle development plans.
·    Manage the assetization process for tooling, collaborating closely with the Finance team to ensure proper capitalization and compliance.
 
VA/VE (Value Analysis/Value Engineering)
•    Collect, track, and report VA/VE proposals from buyers and suppliers, ensuring proper implementation.
•    Support and actively participate in vehicle breakdown events to identify cost-saving opportunities.
•    Engage in internal and external cost optimization meetings, contributing insights and recommendations to enhance cost efficiency.
 
Supplier Reconciliations
·    Oversee the material cost re-evaluation reporting process, ensuring accuracy and compliance.
·    Manage SAP retro-processing and coordinate implementation timelines with the Finance team to ensure seamless execution.
 
Other Responsibilities
•    Support part localization initiatives, ensuring cost-effective sourcing strategies.
•    Lead administrative tasks related to Cost Management, maintaining organized documentation and processes.
•    Assist the Purchasing Management team by gathering, summarizing, and reporting data as needed.
•    Fulfill additional assignments as required, supporting overall department objectives.

HOW WILL YOU MAKE AN IMPACT

HMGMA is the abbreviation of Hyundai Motor Group Metaplant America. It is Hyundai Motor Group's first dedicated EV mass-production plant and is an innovative plant with cutting-edge production technology applied.
