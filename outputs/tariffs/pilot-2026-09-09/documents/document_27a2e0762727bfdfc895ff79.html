Pre-impregnated mechanically bonded fabric of colored glass fiber rovings used as substrate in PCB manufacturing before resin curing. HTS 7019.62.90 covers this as colored closed roving fabric, excluding fully cured composites. Critical for electronics industry.

Alternative Classifications

This product could be classified differently depending on its characteristics or intended use.

7019Same rate: 27%

If woven rather than non-woven

Woven glass fabrics for electrical use classify under 7019.40 even if colored, separate from mechanically bonded non-wovens.

3921.90Lower: 17.3% vs 27%

If fully impregnated and cured

Cured composite sheets with plastic matrix classify as plastic plates under Chapter 39, not glass fabrics.

7019.62Same rate: 27%

If heavier roving construction

Prepregs using rovings 1050+ decitex use 7019.62.20 instead of the 'other' .90 subheading.
