For U.S. defense contracts, buyers should be careful when asking whether Chinese magnet suppliers can provide DFARS compliant magnets. When covered-country restrictions apply, Chinese-origin Samarium Cobalt magnets and Neodymium Iron Boron magnets are generally difficult to treat as DFARS compliant. [...] The 2027 DFARS deadline matters because it moves the issue further upstream. Before that date, the restriction focuses mainly on whether covered magnets are melted or produced in a covered country. Beginning January 1, 2027, the restriction expands toward the broader supply chain, including whether the covered material is mined, refined, separated, melted, or produced in a covered country. [...] The issue is not only the location of the final magnet supplier. Buyers may also need to review the origin of rare earth materials, alloy production, magnet manufacturing, and the complete production route behind the finished magnets.
