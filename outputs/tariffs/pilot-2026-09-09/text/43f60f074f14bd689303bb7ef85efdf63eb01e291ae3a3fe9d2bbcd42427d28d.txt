As negotiations surrounding the future of the United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement (USMCA) continue, the strength of the agreement remains critical to the competitiveness of the North American auto industry. Autos Drive America and six other leading auto industry associations have joined together to support the agreement and highlight its importance to U.S. auto manufacturing.
A strong, trilateral USMCA provides the foundation for an integrated North American auto market; supporting nearly 11 million American jobs, driving hundreds of billions of dollars in investment for U.S. manufacturing, and helping keep vehicles affordable for American consumers. At the same time, the industry faces growing competition from China, whose rapidly expanding auto exports and excess production capacity threaten manufacturers and workers around the world. Maintaining a strong North American market is critical to ensuring the United States can compete globally.
The auto industry has come together to highlight the importance of preserving USMCA and allowing its provisions to deliver the benefits they were designed to provide. Read our coalition document to learn more about USMCA’s role in strengthening U.S. auto manufacturing, supporting American jobs, and keeping the North American auto industry competitive.
