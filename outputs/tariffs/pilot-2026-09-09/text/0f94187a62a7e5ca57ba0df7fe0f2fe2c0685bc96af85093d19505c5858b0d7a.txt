The first decision is whether the battery is **rechargeable** (secondary) or **non-rechargeable** (primary).
**8506: Primary cells and primary batteries** (non-rechargeable). Subheadings split by chemistry:
- 8506.10: Manganese dioxide (alkaline AA/AAA/C/D/9V batteries and coin cells, 2.7%)
- 8506.50: Lithium primary (CR2032 and similar coin cells, 2.7%)
- 8506.80: Other primary cells (silver oxide, zinc air, mercury: 2.7%)
Within each, statistical suffixes break out button/coin cells (.10) from other formats (.90).
**8507: Electric storage batteries (secondary, rechargeable)**. Subheadings split by chemistry:
- 8507.10: Lead-acid, of a kind used for starting piston engines (automotive starter batteries, 3.5%). The 10-digit split distinguishes 12V batteries ≤6 kg (.30, motorcycle/compact car) from >6 kg (.60, standard automotive) from other (.90).
- 8507.20: Other lead-acid (industrial, marine, deep-cycle, UPS: 3.5%). The 10-digit split is by voltage: 6V (.31), 12V (.41), 36V (.61), other (.91).
- 8507.30: Nickel-cadmium (2.5% for the EV-suffix.00; rates vary slightly by suffix)
- 8507.50: Nickel-metal hydride (NiMH, 3.4%)
- 8507.60: **Lithium-ion** (3.4%). Suffix.10 covers EV traction battery packs ("of a kind used as the primary source of electrical power for electrically powered vehicles"); suffix.20 covers other lithium-ion (the bulk of consumer electronics, power tools, and stationary storage).
- 8507.80: Other accumulators (3.4%)
- 8507.90: Battery parts: lead-acid parts (.40 at 3.5%), other parts (.80 at 3.4%)
The **lithium-ion EV vs other split** at 8507.60.00.10 vs.20 matters for both rate (same 3.4% MFN, but Section 301 stacking differs) and statistical reporting under recent EV-related trade measures.
A practical note: batteries integrated into a finished device (a laptop with a built-in battery, a power tool with a battery pack) generally follow the parent device's classification. Standalone batteries (including OEM replacements and aftermarket packs) classify under 8506 / 8507.
**Important duty caveat for batteries**, particularly lithium-ion. Although the MFN rate is 3.4%, Chinese-origin lithium-ion batteries are subject to Section 301 List 4A tariffs that have increased significantly since 2024, and EV traction packs from China are subject to additional measures under the Inflation Reduction Act eligibility framework. Total landed duty on Chinese-origin lithium-ion is materially higher than the base 3.4% rate. Country-specific verification is essential before any prospective entry.
