Quick answer for importers
How do you find the US import duty for electric vehicles?
Start by identifying the correct 10-digit HTS code for the exact electric vehicles product, then apply the base MFN duty and any country-of-origin tariff overlays such as Section 301, Section 232, FTA preference, or AD/CVD exposure. Most electric vehicles classifications begin in HTS Chapter 87 or HTS Chapter 85.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the US tariff on electric vehicles?
Electric vehicles are classified under HTS 8703.80 with a base duty rate of 2.5%. However, Chinese-origin EVs face an additional 100% Section 301 tariff, bringing the effective rate to 102.5%.
Do Section 232 tariffs apply to EVs?
Yes, Section 232 tariffs of 25% apply to automobile imports from countries without specific trade agreements. Combined with Section 301 tariffs, Chinese EVs face some of the highest effective duty rates of any product.
Are EVs from Mexico or Canada duty-free?
EVs from Mexico and Canada can qualify for duty-free treatment under USMCA if they meet the agreement's rules of origin, including a 75% regional value content requirement.
