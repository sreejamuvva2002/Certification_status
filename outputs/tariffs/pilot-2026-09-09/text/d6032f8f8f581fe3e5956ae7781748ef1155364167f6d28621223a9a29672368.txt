HS Code Showdown: Should a mechanical part of a smart phone made of plastics be in chapter 39 or chapter 85? - This is one of the most common debates in trade compliance: Where do purely mechanical plastic mobile phone parts—like casings, buttons, and SIM trays—belong in the HS code system? - The Core Conflict: Chapter 85 (Electrical Parts): Argues the items are parts of a Heading 8517 apparatus, specifically designed for that device. Chapter 39 (Plastics): Argues the items are fundamentally articles of plastic that perform only a passive, mechanical, or structural function. - The Prevailing Classification Answer Based on the General Interpretive Rules (GRI), specifically the exclusions in Section XVI Note 2, items that are themselves specifically described in another Chapter must be classified there. Since a plastic casing is a simple article of plastic, it is typically excluded from the electrical parts headings of Chapter 85. The correct classification is generally found under: ➡️ Chapter 39, Heading 3926 (Other articles of plastics). To classify under 8517.70, the part must be an integral electrical component (like an antenna or a printed circuit board), not just a passive plastic shell. What's your experience?
