9801.00.10
Products of the United States when returned after having been exported, or any other products when returned within 3 years after having been exported, without having been advanced in value or improved in condition by any process of manufacture or other means while abroad
This code details the classification of goods previously exported from the U.S. that are now being returned, whether unchanged or after repair/alteration. Use these provisions to determine duty-free or reduced-duty treatment for returned U.S. goods, found within Chapter 98, specifically subchapter I, and understand any applicable conditions or restrictions.
This section of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule covers articles that were exported from the United States and are now being returned, either in the same condition or after undergoing repairs or alterations, under specific conditions outlined in the notes. The code 9801.00 specifically addresses goods returned without having been further manufactured or improved, encompassing a very detailed list of subdivisions by specific product classifications. These subdivisions allow for precise statistical reporting, and the notes emphasize that statistical suffixes should be used to identify the original classification of the item when determining duty rates, particularly if the item was previously subject to internal revenue tax or drawback. Importers should carefully review the extensive list of subdivisions and accompanying notes to select the correct statistical suffix based on the product's original classification and any applicable tax or drawback considerations.

The main code 9801.00 covers articles exported and returned, not advanced or improved in condition, and generally receives duty-free treatment as detailed in the US Notes. This means no duty is assessed on these returned goods, provided they meet specific conditions like not being exported with drawback or involving certain bonded warehouse situations. The numerous subheadings (9801.00.10 through 9801.00.99) further specify the types of goods covered within this duty-free framework, offering granularity for statistical reporting. No specific rate is specified for any of the subheadings because they fall under the general duty-free provision of 9801.00; however, statistical reporting is required, with units varying depending on the specific article being reported as noted in the chapter notes. The unit of quantity is not specified but needs to match the original provision from which the rate was derived.
