For automobiles assembled in the United States between May 1, 2026, through April 30, 2027, OEMs will be eligible to receive 2.5 percent of the aggregate MSRP value. These percentages reflect the total duty that would be owed when a 25 percent duty is applied to parts accounting 15% (2025-26) and 10% (2026-27) of the automobile’s MSRP value, respectively.
