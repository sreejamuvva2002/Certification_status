SAO PAULO (ICIS)–The US and Argentina have ratified the trade deal agreed in November which is set to eliminate tariffs on hundreds of products while expanding US beef access to the South American nation.

Washington’s primary focus was on securing investments in critical minerals, including lithium and copper, a theme which has kept the US Administration very busy this week. [...] The US-Argentina Agreement on Reciprocal Trade and Investment removes Argentine tariffs on 221 product categories, including machinery, transport equipment, some chemicals, medical devices and auto parts, which will enter duty-free or face reduced levies of 2%, according to the official text.

See this PDF document detailing all US import rates – or lack of – to Argentina’s products. [...] In exchange, the US will eliminate reciprocal tariffs imposed earlier this year on certain Argentine goods, particularly natural resources and pharmaceutical inputs.

Argentina will also expand its beef import quota from the US to 80,000 metric tonnes in 2026, distributed in quarterly tranches of 20,000 tonnes each, the agreement states.

The deal additionally creates quotas for 10,000 US motor vehicles and smaller amounts of cheese, wine, almonds and other agricultural products.
