After months of talks spurred by U.S. tariff actions, the United States and Japan announced in July 2025 a bilateral "strategic trade and investment agreement." As part of the so-called framework agreement, the United States imposed a 15% tariff on most imports from Japan, a reduction from the 25% that President Trump initially proposed but an increase from 2024 U.S. tariff rates.
