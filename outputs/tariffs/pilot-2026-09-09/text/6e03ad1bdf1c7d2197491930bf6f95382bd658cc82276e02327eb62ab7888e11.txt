Frequently Asked Questions
What is the US tariff on lithium-ion batteries?
Lithium-ion batteries are classified under HTS 8507.60 with a base duty rate of 3.4%. Section 301 tariffs on Chinese-origin lithium-ion batteries add 25%, bringing the effective rate to 28.4% for Chinese imports.
Are EV batteries subject to additional tariffs?
Yes, EV battery cells and packs from China face Section 301 tariffs of 25% on top of the base 3.4% duty. This has accelerated battery production shifts to South Korea, Japan, and domestic US facilities.
What HTS code is used for batteries?
Batteries fall under HTS 8507 (electric accumulators). Lithium-ion is 8507.60, lead-acid is 8507.10, nickel-metal hydride is 8507.50. Each has different base duty rates ranging from 2.7% to 3.4%.
