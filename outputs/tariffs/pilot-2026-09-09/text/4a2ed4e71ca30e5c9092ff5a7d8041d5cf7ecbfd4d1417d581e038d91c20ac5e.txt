The U.S. Customs & Border Protection (CBP) published a Notice that would revoke the Agency’s previous rulings on lithium ion cells in favor of classifying cells in the U.S. harmonized tariff schedule (HTS) applicable to batteries. Until 2018, the CBP’s tariff classification position on cells and batteries was of little practical importance because there was no difference in the duties applicable to imports under any of the applicable codes.
