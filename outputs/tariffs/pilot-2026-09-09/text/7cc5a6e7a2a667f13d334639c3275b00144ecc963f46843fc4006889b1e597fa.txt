Commodity:
Automobiles from the United Kingdom (UK) in the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), Chapter 87 subheading, specified in Executive Order 14309, “Implementing the General Terms of the United States of America-United Kingdom Economic Prosperity Deal,” 90 FR 26419 (June 23, 2025).
Quota Periods:
January 1, 2026 – March 31, 2026
April 1, 2026 – June 30, 2026
Opening Dates:
Friday, January 2, 2026
Wednesday, April 1, 2026
All entries submitted after 12:01 am local port time and prior to 08:30 am Eastern Time (ET) and on the opening date will receive an entry time of 08:30 am for quota qualification purposes. If the total for any HTSUS group exceeds the limit at 08:30 am ET, all accepted entries will be prorated.
Restraint Levels:
Pursuant to Executive Order 14309 of June 16, 2025 “Implementing the General Terms of the United States of America-United Kingdom Economic Prosperity Deal,” 90 FR 26419 (June 23, 2025) imports of automobiles that are the products of the UK within the tariff rate quota are subject to an annual limit of 100,000 automobiles. The quota will be administered quarterly pursuant to the Annex guidance published in the Federal Register.
Imports of automobiles within the tariff rate quota that would otherwise be subject to a 25 percent tariff under Proclamation 10908 of March 26, 2025 “Adjusting Imports of Automobiles and Automobile Parts into the United States,” shall instead be subject to a 7.5 percent tariff under Executive Order 140309.
The first and second quarter limits are 25,000 automobiles each. U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) shall carry forward any unused quantity of automobiles that are products of the UK from the first quarter to the third quarter and shall carry forward any unused quantity from the second quarter to the fourth quarter. Articles entered in excess of quarterly limit totals are subject to all applicable tariffs.

Reporting Instructions:
The automobiles subject to the tariff rate quota are exempt from the reciprocal tariffs pursuant to Executive Order 14257. Filers should report 9903.01.33 to claim exemption.
The automobiles subject to the tariff rate quota are also subject to the stacking provisions of Executive Order 14289, “Addressing Certain Tariffs on Imported Articles.”
For in-quota merchandise, use entry type code 02, 06, 07, 12, 23, 32, 38, or 52.
Filers must use HTSUS number 9903.94.31 as the first HTSUS number, and then the corresponding in-quota chapter 87 HTSUS number.
For over-quota merchandise, use a non-quota entry type and 9903.94.01 with the appropriate HTSUS chapter 87 number.
Quota entries will be processed on a “first come, first served” fashion based on presentation date/time until the limit for that quota ID HTSUS group is reached.
A presentation date/time is determined for the entry summary when the last of the following conditions is met:
- Error-free entry summary on file.
- Entry summary payment or statement scheduled information transmitted to the Automated Commercial Environment (ACE).
- Shipment arrival information at the intended port of unlading transmitted to ACE.
The unit of measure is Numbers (No).
Merchandise subject to this tariff rate quota may also be subject to other duties (Antidumping/Countervailing, Section 232, etc.).
