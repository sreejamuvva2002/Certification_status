Compared to 2024, GPA saw a 7.5% decline in 2025 volume at the Port of Brunswick, its Roll-On/Roll-Off port for autos and heavy machinery. The drop in volume was driven by tariffs that led to some manufacturers pausing shipments and a decline in exports to China, according to GPA. [...] GPA moved 5.7 million twenty-foot equivalent container units of cargo in 2025, a 2.6% increase over the previous year, the authority reported Tuesday. GPA leaders cited customers diverting cargo from West Coast ports to the East Coast, and months of shippers “front loading” cargo as contributing to the strong volume. [...] The growth was anchored by early year gains in February, March and April, when shippers were moving large amounts of cargo ahead of the anticipated tariffs. Those same gains contributed to GPA having its second busiest fiscal year on record, although tariffs caused dips in volume in June and October.
