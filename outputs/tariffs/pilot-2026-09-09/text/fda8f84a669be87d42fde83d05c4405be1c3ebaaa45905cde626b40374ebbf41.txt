Preliminary Countervailing Duty Investigations of Brake Drums from the People’s Republic of China and the Republic of Türkiye
Preliminary Affirmative Determinations in the Countervailing Duty Investigations of Brake Drums from the People’s Republic of China and the Republic of Türkiye
On November 26, 2024, the U.S. Department of Commerce (Commerce) announced its preliminary affirmative determination in the countervailing duty (CVD) investigation of brake drums from the People’s Republic of China (China) and the Republic of Türkiye (Türkiye). Commerce is also conducting a concurrent antidumping duty (AD) investigation of brake drums from China and Türkiye.
