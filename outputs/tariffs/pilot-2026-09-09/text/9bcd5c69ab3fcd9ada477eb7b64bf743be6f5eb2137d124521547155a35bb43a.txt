### Auto Glass Association

6 May 2025  ·

U.S. Tariffs Hit the US Auto Glass Industry Hard

As the 25% import tariff on vehicles continues and new tariffs on auto parts take effect from May 3, the impact on the auto glass and repair industry is starting to bite. In a recent CIECA webinar, Mitchell’s Ryan Mandell outlined how the evolving trade environment is reshaping parts pricing and repair strategies—especially for glass and ADAS components. [...] With many aftermarket windshields sourced from China, auto glass is directly in the crosshairs. Mandell warns that while full vehicle prices may rise mid-year, glass and ADAS component prices could spike sooner.

Repairers are urged to audit their parts sourcing, anticipate price jumps, and consider more repairs over replacements where possible. The global supply chain isn't going back to "normal" anytime soon—being prepared is the best strategy.
