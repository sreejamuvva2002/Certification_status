As a result of these reviews, the USDOC will impose substantial anti-dumping duties: 90.83% on China, 44.40% on India, 47.86% on Indonesia, 20.30% on Thailand, 90.33% on Ukraine, and 29.14% on Taiwan. Additionally, countervailing duties will be applied to India at 336.62% to 360.23%, Indonesia at 10.21%, and Thailand at 2.38%.
These measures took effect on November 12, 2024, as part of ongoing efforts to protect U.S. industries from unfair trade practices related to steel imports.
