Annex III of the proclamation provides temporary relief for certain plastics machinery, parts, and molds, including specified Harmonized Tariff Schedule (HTS) lines 8477 and 8480. For these products, a combined duty cap of 15% will apply through Dec. 31, 2027. Starting Jan. 1, 2028, Annex III products will transition to standard tariff treatment outlined in the proclamation.
