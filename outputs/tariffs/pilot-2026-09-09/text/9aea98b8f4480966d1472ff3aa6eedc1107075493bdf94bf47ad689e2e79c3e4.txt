Final Determinations in Antidumping and Countervailing Duty Investigations of Common Alloy Aluminum Sheet from 18 Countries
On March 2, 2021, the Department of Commerce (Commerce) announced its affirmative final determinations in the antidumping duty (AD) investigations of common alloy aluminum sheet from Bahrain, Brazil, Croatia, Egypt, Germany, India, Indonesia, Italy, Oman, Romania, Serbia, Slovenia, South Africa, Spain, Taiwan, and Turkey, and affirmative final determinations in the countervailing duty (CVD) investigations of common alloy aluminum sheet from Bahrain, India, and Turkey. Commerce also announced negative determinations in the AD investigations of common alloy aluminum sheet from Greece and South Korea and the CVD investigation of common alloy aluminum sheet from Brazil.
