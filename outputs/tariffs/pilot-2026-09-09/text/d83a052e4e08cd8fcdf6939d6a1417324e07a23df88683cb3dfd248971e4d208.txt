Cobalt(II) Sulfate Heptahydrate
Cobalt(II) sulfate heptahydrate (CoSO4·7H2O) is a pink crystalline salt used in electroplating baths and as a cobalt source in fertilizers. It falls under HTS 2833.29.10.00 as a sulfate of cobalt, classified among other sulfates of metals per Chapter 28 notes.
