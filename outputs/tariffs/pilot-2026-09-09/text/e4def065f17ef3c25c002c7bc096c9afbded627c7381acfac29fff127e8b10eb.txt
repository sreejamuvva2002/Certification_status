Frequently Asked Questions
What is the US tariff on auto parts?
Auto parts duty rates vary widely from 0% to 6% depending on the component. Section 232 tariffs of 25% apply to certain auto parts, particularly those from countries without trade agreements.
Are auto parts from Mexico duty-free under USMCA?
Auto parts from Mexico can enter duty-free under USMCA if they meet the rules of origin requirements, including regional value content thresholds of 75% for core parts.
What HTS chapter covers automobile parts?
Most auto parts fall under HTS Chapter 87 (8708 specifically). However, engines are in 8407-8408, tires in 4011, batteries in 8507, and electronics in Chapter 85. Classification depends on the specific component.
What tariffs apply to auto parts from South Korea?
Auto Parts imported from South Korea are subject to the standard MFN base duty rate, plus Section 232 steel screening, Section 232 aluminum screening tariff overlays. Use the duty calculator to get the exact rate for your HTS code.
How do I calculate the landed cost for auto parts from South Korea?
The landed cost includes the product value (FOB), freight, insurance, applicable US Customs duties, Merchandise Processing Fee (0.3464% of value, subject to the $33.58–$651.50 band as of 2025-10-01), and Harbor Maintenance Fee (0.125% for applicable ocean shipments).
