Georgia is quickly becoming a national leader in electric and hybrid vehicle manufacturing due to the well-established automotive industry. Since 2018, advanced mobility investments have steadily increased, anchored by major projects from Kia, Hyundai Motor Group, and Rivian. Georgia offers an end-to-end supply chain for vehicle assembly, advanced battery, and materials, supporting innovation, scalability, and long-term growth in electrification.
