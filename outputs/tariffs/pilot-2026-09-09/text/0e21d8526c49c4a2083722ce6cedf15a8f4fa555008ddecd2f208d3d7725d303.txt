However, in several previous decisions, CBP has addressed the issue of disjunctive multiple country of origin marking requirements with regard to integrated circuits. In T.D. 75-187, dated July 22,1975, CBP allowed a disjunctive listing of multiple countries of origin when the articles (semiconductor devices, including transistors, diodes, and integrated circuits) were commingled for a bona fide reason, and subsequently repackaged for sale to an ultimate purchaser.
