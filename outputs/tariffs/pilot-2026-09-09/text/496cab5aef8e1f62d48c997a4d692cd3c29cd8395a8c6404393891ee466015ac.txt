As CBP considers the totality of circumstances in its substantial transformation analysis, considerations such as the origin of a PCBA may be taken into account together with the nature of the overall assembly operations. Indeed, in several matters, CBP has determined that the PCBA provides the character of the subject devices and, accordingly, the country of origin is that in which the PCBA is manufactured.
