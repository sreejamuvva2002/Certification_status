SAVANNAH, Ga. — One of the nation’s busiest ports says 2026 is off to a slower start, but leaders are cautiously optimistic.
The Port of Savannah just saw its second busiest year on record, but tariffs are adding a new challenge. The port is trying to expand relationships with many countries that are now under new tariffs by President Donald Trump.
But the overall outlook for this year is still trending positive.
“We’re very hopeful that this summer, not only with the trade deals coming together, some of our economists that we talk with a work with and our customers are hopeful that we’ll see some better volumes in the summer going into 2027,” said Griff Lynch, Georgia Ports Authority president and CEO.
Lynch says their auto exports dropped considerably when it comes to China.
