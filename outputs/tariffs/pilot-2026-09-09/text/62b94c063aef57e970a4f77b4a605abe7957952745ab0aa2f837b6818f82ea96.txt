Binding classification advice can only be given by the Office of Regulations and Rulings. The importer submits either an electronic ruling request or a letter describing the product in detail and provides a sample to the CBP Information Exchange, National Commodity Specialist for a ruling. Tariff Classifications are binding, however duty rates are not. The program promotes compliance, uniformity and accuracy. [...] The Binding Ruling Program enables importers and other interested parties to get binding pre-entry classification decisions prior to importing a product and filing entries with Customs and Border Protection (CBP). It is also to get binding advance ruling and other legal decision in connection with the importation of merchandise into the United States.
