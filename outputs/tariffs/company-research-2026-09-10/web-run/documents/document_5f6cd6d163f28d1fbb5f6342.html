Commercial Vehicles That Work as Hard as You Do

Founded in 1952 and still family owned and operated, Fouts Bros Inc. is one of the largest and most diverse commercial truck, van, and automotive equipment manufacturers in the U.S. Over the last seven decades, we’ve grown from a single service station in Smyrna, GA, into a thriving collection of industrial brands, each focused on manufacturing smart, efficient apparatus to help keep our fire, fleet, service, and military customers across the country on the move and ready to serve.

About Fouts Bros Inc.

Fouts Bros Inc. is a family-owned company of brands all dedicated to the manufacturing and upfitting of excellent American work trucks for a variety of private and public sectors. We’re based out of our 700,000 square foot headquarters in Milledgeville, GA, with a second facility in La Grange, TX.
