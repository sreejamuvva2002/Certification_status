Bosch Revives Challenge to Duties on Vehicle Computer Component

Jan. 31, 2020, 7:34 PM UTC
Brian Flood
Editor
A Robert Bosch GmbH electric drive system sits on display during day two of the IAA Frankfurt Motor Show in Frankfurt, Germany, on Wednesday, Sept. 11, 2019.
Krisztian Bocsi/Bloomberg via Getty Images
  • COURT: Ct. Int’l Trade
  • TRACK DOCKET: No. 1:20-cv-00028

Robert Bosch LLC has filed a new lawsuit arguing that its imports of a component used in dashboard computers in vehicles was misclassified and should escape import duties, after the company dropped a similar lawsuit last month.

Bosch’s “human/machine interface controllers” allow a driver or passenger to use a menu-driven touch screen to control various functions like the vehicle’s heat and air conditioning, audio entertainment, media devices, and externally mounted cameras, according to the company’s complaint in its previous lawsuit.
