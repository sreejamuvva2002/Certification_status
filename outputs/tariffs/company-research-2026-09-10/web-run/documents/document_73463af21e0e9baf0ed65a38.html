About the Company

With around 2,300 dedicated employees at 18 locations in 8 countries on 4 continents, the LINDE + WIEMANN Group is a worldwide renowned partner in the automotive industry. We are producing structural components for the vehicle construction, including Sills, A- and B-Pillars as well as structural components in the Front End, Cockpit- and Chassis field.

Our products are based on a high level of understanding of the processes used in the field of stamping, profile, hot forming and joining technology.

LINDE + WIEMANN applies the know-how, gained through many years of experience, to design and form metal to find the right trendsetting solutions tailor-made to suit our customers’ requirements. L+W focuses on developing new products that improve the safety of vehicles while reducing their weight at the same time.

Mission Statement of LINDE + WIEMANN

For more than 80 years, we have been supplying complex and structurally critical components and assemblies to vehicle manufacturers and suppliers around the world. We are known for our high quality, renowned for our interdisciplinary thinking and appreciated for our values. We are focused on our mission to be our customers’ first global partner for high-quality body components that are relevant in crash situations, and we have set clear goals for many more successful years to come.

Preferred strategic supplier and development partner to premium OEM customers through our product development and excellent manufacturing capabilities in close cooperation with our customers.

Contributing towards improving the safety of vehicles while reducing cost and weight for innovative Body-in-White, crash and safety relevant structural components and technology solutions e.g. for fast growing new BEV platforms.

Enabler of eMobility and sustainability with CO2 friendly roll-forming technology and best practice CSR / ESG system.

Attractive place for international team to work for with both high-performance and entrepreneurial teams and culture of team spirit, trust, transparency, work ethics, diversity – and high quality of work life.

Global footprint to serve our key OEM customers in relevant growth markets and regions in Western and Eastern Europe, North America, Asia and Africa.

Sustainable and profitable growth to allow investments in product development projects in close cooperation with our customers and in high technology machines and equipment for highly efficient global manufacturing facilities.

We are using our decades of expertise in metal design and forming to find the right forward-thinking solutions tailored to our customers’ needs. With our strategy, we will create more value for our customers in the future, in a sustainable and responsible manner for the environment and the people who stand for LINDE + WIEMANN.

Diese Cookies sind unbedingt erforderlich, um Ihnen die über unsere Website verfügbaren Dienstleistungen zur Verfügung zu stellen und einige ihrer Funktionen zu nutzen.

Da diese Cookies für die Bereitstellung der Website unbedingt erforderlich sind, hat ihre Ablehnung Auswirkungen auf die Funktionsweise unserer Website. Sie können Cookies jederzeit blockieren oder löschen, indem Sie Ihre Browsereinstellungen ändern und die Blockierung aller Cookies auf dieser Website erzwingen. Dies wird Sie jedoch immer dazu auffordern, Cookies zu akzeptieren/abzulehnen, wenn Sie unsere Website erneut besuchen.

Wir respektieren es voll und ganz, wenn Sie Cookies ablehnen möchten, aber um zu vermeiden, dass Sie immer wieder gefragt werden, erlauben Sie uns bitte, dafür ein Cookie zu speichern. Es steht Ihnen frei, sich jederzeit abzumelden oder sich für andere Cookies zu entscheiden, um ein besseres Erlebnis zu erhalten. Wenn Sie Cookies ablehnen, werden wir alle gesetzten Cookies in unserer Domäne entfernen.

Wir stellen Ihnen eine Liste der auf Ihrem Computer in unserer Domäne gespeicherten Cookies zur Verfügung, damit Sie überprüfen können, was wir gespeichert haben. Aus Sicherheitsgründen ist es uns nicht möglich, Cookies von anderen Domains anzuzeigen oder zu ändern. Sie können diese in den Sicherheitseinstellungen Ihres Browsers überprüfen.
