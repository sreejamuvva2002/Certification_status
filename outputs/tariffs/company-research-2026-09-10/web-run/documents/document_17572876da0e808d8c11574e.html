As a reliable partner, we provide planning and delivery reliability throughout the entire product lifecycle—from co-engineering and series ramp-up to global scaling. Automotive Components

Automotive Components is a business unit in the Metal Forming Division. Our core competence lies in the development and implementation of light-weight solutions for the automotive industry. As a globally active network, we manufacture innovative parts made of steel and other metals for international customers. With roughly 4,600 employees and 14 production sites worldwide, we generate a sales volume of around 1,300 million euros in Europe, China, the United States and South Africa.
