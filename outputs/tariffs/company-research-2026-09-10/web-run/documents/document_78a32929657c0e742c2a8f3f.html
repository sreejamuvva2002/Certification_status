CEO인사말

One question mark was the starting point of today's Daehan Solution.

"Why should people talk louder in cars driving at over 100km/h than those who are walking?"


Welcome to Daehan Solution hompage.

Since its foundation in 1982, Daehan Solution prioritized human, environment, and customer satisfaction to provide Total System Engineering, which includes N.V.H. of automobiles, ranging from testing, diagnosis and prescribed design to production for reducing internal and external noise in relation to automobile noise and vibration.

We are not satisfied with the improvement of ride quality by simply reducing internal and external noise of automobiles, but we continue endless research and development in order to present satisfaction beyond comfort to all customers by redesigning rough noise into pleasant sound.


President Kwon Hoehyeon

CI

01Symbol Mark
The symbol mark is a design representing Corporate Identity, which plays the most important role in creating a unified image as the public symbol that integrates the corporate image of Daehan Solution.
The first letter of English ‘D’ and ‘arrow (→)’ are combined to form a figure. It contains the will of Daehan Solutions to develop based on harmony and stability amid the changes and growth of the global economy in the 21st century.
Arrows signify challenge and creation for the future. The letter 'D' is used to express the development in stability and symbolizes harmony. Red symbolizes initiative and black symbolizes excellence and dignity.
02 Logo Type

The symbol mark is a design representing Corporate Identity, which plays the most important role in creating a unified image as the public symbol that integrates the corporate image of Daehan Solution.

03 Signature
The signature is a combination of symbol mark and logo type in an ideal proportion.
Since it is a combination of the two most representative visual elements of Daehan Solution's Corporate Identity, the combinations of the appointed design can only be used, and cannot be changed.
The signature is designed in Korean, English, vertical and horizontal combinations, and the appropriate design can be selected depending on the situation. Use the provided image file after zooming it or out.

04 Identity Color
The identity color is one of the important elements of Corporate Identity that cannot be disregarded.
It is used to express unique color of symbol mark and signature of Daehan Solution.
The standard color in the manual should be applied after reviewing the printing method, ink concentration, and paper quality.
