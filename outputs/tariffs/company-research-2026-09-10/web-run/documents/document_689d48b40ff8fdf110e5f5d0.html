SOLVAY SPECIALTY POLYMERS USA LLC

SOLVAY SPECIALTY POLYMERS USA LLC is a U.S. importer with 2,035 bill-of-lading shipment records between Nov 2019 and Oct 2024. It is the largest U.S. importer of Vinylidene chloride polymers (HS 390450) by shipment count. Its top suppliers include SOLVIN FRANCE, SOLVAY SPECIALTY POLYMERS ITALY S P A, and SOLVAY SPECIALTY POLYMERS ITALY SPA, primarily importing HS code 390450. Goods originate mainly from BELGIUM and ITALY, arriving primarily at NEW YORK/NEWARK AREA, NEWARK, NJ. Shipment volume has held steady over the past 12 months versus the prior year.
