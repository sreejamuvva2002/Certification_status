Global Presence

 
Home > Company > Global Presence

At Hollingsworth & Vose (H&V), we are a manufacturer of advanced materials for filtration, energy and industrial applications. Over our more than 100 years in business, our company has undergone numerous expansion efforts that have ultimately led to us being a trusted source for materials in various markets across the world. Today, we maintain 13 facilities in strategic locations across three continents, North America, Europe and Asia – a footprint that lets us serve the unique requirements of global and regional customers efficiently, accurately and cost effectively.

Click here to learn more about our global presence

Business at a Global Level

Equipped with an extensive international footprint, we are well-prepared to conduct operations on a global scale. In each of our locations, we aim to hire locally to raise new opportunities to further our commitment to these regions. We focus on developing solid relationships with local businesses and community members to ensure full acceptance.

Whether we enter a new market or one of our existing markets matures, we will support its growth. Whether we gain new customers or our existing customers relocate, we will adjust and align our operations to meet their demands. That’s what makes us a global leader in the supply of advanced materials.

This established global presence makes a significant impact on the efficiencies of the international supply chain. This vertical integration we possess assists with the improvement of costs when it comes to the sourcing and distribution of products, among other extensive cost savings. H&V is not only set up to achieve a more economic operation but also allows full control when changes and enhancements are necessary.

Business at a global level requires proper accordance and compliance. Here you can view our certifications for each of our locations.

How We Got Here

Hollingsworth & Vose opened with a single location in East Walpole, Massachusetts. While we established new facilities over the years to expand our domestic and, eventually, international capabilities, this one remains our company headquarters.

Some of our key expansion efforts include:

  • Erecting a facility in New York state in the 1950s
  • Building a facility in Virginia in the 1970s
  • Forming a joint venture in Mexico in 1981
  • Establishing a presence in the United Kingdom while simultaneously creating new facilities in the southern and northwestern United States from the 1980s to the 2000s
  • Constructing facilities in Germany throughout the 2000s
  • Opening a facility in Suzhou, China in 2004

With each of these milestones, we further cemented our status as a global business. While we already have a presence on three continents, we are ready and willing to expand when the opportunity arises and the market calls for it. In the meantime, we will focus on improving operations in our current facilities to ensure our customers continue to be satisfied and our company continues to thrive and prosper.

If you’re looking for an advanced materials supplier with a history of technology and a vision of innovation, H&V is the ideal partner. Since Zachery Hollingsworth and Charles Vose formed their partnership in 1881, the company has continually strived for evolution and expansion. Today, we utilize our 15 R&D and manufacturing facilities strategically placed across the globe to develop and deliver advanced materials that contribute to a cleaner world. Our products commonly find use in devices and systems that provide clean air/liquids and energy storage.
