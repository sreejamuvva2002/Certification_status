Company Overview

Enchem America INC., a subsidiary of South Korean-based Enchem Co., Ltd., specializes in manufacturing electrolytes and high-functionality additives for rechargeable batteries. Founded to develop essential components for electric vehicles and energy storage systems, it is headquartered in Commerce, Georgia, and has expanded operations to Tennessee to optimize supply chain management and meet market demand.

Battery Technology

Advanced electrolytes and high-functionality additives for rechargeable batteries, focusing on high-performance lithium-ion electrolytes for automotive applications; N-Methyl-2-pyrrolidone (NMP) recycling solutions; carbon nanotube (CNT) conductive slurry dispersion technology; specialized mixing equipment and automated electrolyte production lines. Manufacturing processes feature strict quality control and continuous R&D for next-generation EV requirements.

Products

High-performance electrolytes for lithium-ion automotive batteries; high-functionality additives; NMP recycling solutions; CNT conductive slurry dispersions; specialized mixing equipment and production lines for electrolyte manufacturing.

Recent Projects

2023: $152.5 million facility expansion in Brownsville, Tennessee, creating 190 jobs to increase production capacity for Southeast EV manufacturers; supply agreement with AESC covering 70% of electrolyte needs for lithium iron phosphate battery production in China and North America.
