Another South Korea-based company, Woory Industrial Co., is also headed to the state, investing $18 million to establish its first manufacturing facility in the U.S.

The new plant will be located in Dublin and will produce electrical heaters, control units and actuators for EV and internal combustion automakers in North America, according to a June 7 announcement. The site will create more than 130 jobs and operations are expected to commence in November.
