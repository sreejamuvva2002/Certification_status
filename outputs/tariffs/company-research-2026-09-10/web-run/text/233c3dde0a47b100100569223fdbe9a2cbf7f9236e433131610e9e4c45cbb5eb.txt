Press Releases
Adairsville, Georgia April 30, 2024
Vista Metals Georgia is pleased to announce the completion of its 7th cast-house, a significant milestone in the company’s ongoing $100 million long-range capital plan. This expansion represents the second phase of the company’s plan and includes the installation of melting furnaces, homogenizing ovens, and support equipment at its Adairsville, Georgia facility.
Kelly Thomas, President of Vista Metals Georgia, announced that
“Vista’s investment in this expansion will further support the growth in the core markets we serve, including outer space, commercial aviation, defense and industrial applications. Added to this, we are completing final equipment installation in our new world class finishing facility which is a critical part of this expansion phase.”
Vista Metals previously announced on March 19, 2024 their investment in a new location to serve the aerospace industry with specialty aluminum products. This new facility, located in Bowling Green, Kentucky, is part of Vista Metals’ long range investment plan, and will expand upon Vista Metals’ existing capabilities in California and Georgia.
