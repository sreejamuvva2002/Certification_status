Join the Sewon America Team
Sewon America is proud to be the largest automotive body parts manufacturing plant in North America, renowned for delivering high-quality products through cutting-edge automated manufacturing processes and efficient logistics.
In 2023, we expanded our commitment to excellence by breaking ground on a new state-of-the-art automotive manufacturing plant in Rincon, Georgia. This facility, set to begin production in 2024, will further strengthen our international presence as our second plant in the United States.
Once fully operational, the Rincon plant will have created 740 new jobs, offering exciting opportunities for individuals who want to be part of a growing international company dedicated to innovation and quality.
