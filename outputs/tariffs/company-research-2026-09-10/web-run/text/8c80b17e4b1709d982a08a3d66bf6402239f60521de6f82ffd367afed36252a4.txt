WHAT WE DO
ALBAform provides manufacturing solutions. We master sheet metal bending techniques, CNC wire forming, welding, laser profiling, metal fabrication and sheet metal assembly for the automotive, agriculture, and e‑mobility sectors.
INTERIOR PARTS FOR AUTOMOTIVE SEATING
- We manufacture automotive components for car seating: metal structures for seat cushions, headrests, center consoles, and door panels.
- We are an automotive interior supplier producing custom wire reinforcements for attaching the textile and leather upholstery in automotive seats, dashboard reinforcements, wire harness assemblies bending for car parts, and other car seat manufacturing.
WELDMENTS FOR HEAVY INDUSTRIES
- Our custom weldments consist of wires, tubes, profiles, and stamped or laser-cut metal sheets while ensuring precise measurements and quality.
- We produce:
  - welded tapered arms for light duty front loading tractors
  - steering support for lawnmowers
  - transportation containers for industrial gases, CNG, and hydrogen
  - heavy metal and large metal fabrication
  - metalworking solutions, design, and fabrication
AUTOMATED ASSEMBLIES
As wire assembly manufacturers, we use our expertise to develop most cost-effective processes for our customers:
- Combination of bending, stamping, and assembly of steel and plastic parts — using benefits of both materials
- Using High-Tech Fanuc robotics for handling, manipulation, forming, and bending
