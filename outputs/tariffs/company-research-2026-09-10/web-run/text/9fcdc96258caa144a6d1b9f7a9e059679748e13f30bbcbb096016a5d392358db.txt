About us
We began our journey in 1988 as SW Manufacturing, Inc.; the first international manufacturing facility for Shiroki Corporation (our Parent Company) taking advantage of Shiroki senior management's "international foresight". We grew from one plant (in Smithville, TN) to two (Dalton, GA) in 1995; then later to three (Gordonsville, TN) late 2006. In 2007 Shiroki Corporation decided for better global management and branding purposes, that SW Manufacturing would be renamed SHIROKI North America, Inc.; establishing a portion of the Smithville, TN site to function as the Headquarters for all North America business. At the end of 2014 it was announced that AISIN SEIKI (Japan) would subsume Shiroki Corporation (and thus SHIROKI North America, Inc.); which was concluded as of April 2016. Now AISIN SHIROKI Corporation (our parent company), an AISIN Group company, we are energized and working towards our better tomorrow each day.
