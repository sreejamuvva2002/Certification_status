Bonnell Aluminum Commenting on Preliminary Countervailing Duty Ruling
March 6, 2024 8:30 AM Eastern Standard Time
Newnan, GA – (BUSINESS WIRE) – Bonnell Aluminum, a subsidiary of Tredegar Corporation (NYSE: TG), is pleased with the U.S. Department of Commerce’s ruling to institute preliminary countervailing duties on unfairly traded imports of aluminum extrusions.
