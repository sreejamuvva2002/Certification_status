TCI Powder Coatings is a manufacturer and distributer of premier thermoset powder coatings for the Architectural, Heavy Duty Equipment, Automotive, TruCoater: Custom Coater, Furniture and Fixtures, Appliance and General Industrial markets.
Founded in Ellaville, GA with a modest plant and a portable building that served as the lab and office space, TCI has since expanded to become an international company with multiple warehouse locations, a state-of-the-art product development center, and a staff of several hundred employees focused on servicing our customers.
