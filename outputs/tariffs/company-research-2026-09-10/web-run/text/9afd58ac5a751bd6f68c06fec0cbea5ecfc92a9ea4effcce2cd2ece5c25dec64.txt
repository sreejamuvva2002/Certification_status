# About

GSC Steel Stamping, LLC, is a minority and family-owned and operated automotive parts manufacturer providing Body in White frame assembly brackets to some of the world’s leading OEMs and tier one suppliers.  GSC Steel Stamping, LLC offers competitively priced metal stamping, engineering, welded assemblies, and tooling services. [...] In June 2023, GSC acquired Dixien, LLC’s assets, continuing the 43-year history of automotive parts stamping at its 200,000 sq. ft., Lake City, GA location.  Prior to GSC Steel Stamping, CEO, Vincent Henderson, purchased and led the turn-around of two award-winning automotive stamping operations in Michigan, that were regularly recognized, by customers, for excellence in overall performance, quality, and on-time delivery.   This same commitment to excellence persists at GSC, as the company [...] GSC has a very experienced and dedicated staff.  The plant operations management team, alone, possesses over 200 years of combined stamping experience and takes pride in consistently delivering high-quality, precision parts on time.  

## Mission

Our company’s mission is simply, to provide quality parts, shipped on time, every time.
