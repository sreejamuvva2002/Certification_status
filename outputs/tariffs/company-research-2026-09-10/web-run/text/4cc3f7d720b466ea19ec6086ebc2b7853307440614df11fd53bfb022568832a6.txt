About us
Your partner in design, manufacturing & assembly. Established in 1975, Thomson Plastics, Inc. (TPI) has developed processes that integrate the complexity of design with steady and reliable production. Our engineers, managers and production staff allow us to serve a broad range of markets, providing our expertise in logistics management, quality control and just-in-time delivery to every project. TPI has three facilities located in Thomson, GA., Howell, MI, and Lexington, NC. Our plastic injection molding capacity ranges from 60 tons to 3,150 tons. Please visit our website at thomsonplastics.com or drop us an email at sales@thomsonplastics.com.
