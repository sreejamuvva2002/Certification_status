About us
SECO, a leading auto parts manufacturing company in South Korea, was established in 1966. With 8 different auto parts affiliates around the globe, ECOPLASTIC emerged as a significant affiliate in June 1984. ECOPLASTIC specializes in the design, development, and production of exterior and interior auto parts for the global automobile industry. As an exclusive 1-tier supplier to Hyundai (HMGMA), ECOPLASTIC AMERICA now extends its innovative leadership in eco-friendly electronic auto parts manufacturing to North America, capitalizing on cutting-edge technologies and a strong reputation for reliability. For more information, visit: www.ecoplasticamerica.com
