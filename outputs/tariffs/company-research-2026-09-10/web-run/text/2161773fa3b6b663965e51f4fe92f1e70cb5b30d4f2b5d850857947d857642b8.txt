Tariff Information
Update 2 September 2025
In summer 2025, a UK–US trade deal came into effect, setting the tariffs on cars imported into the United States from the United Kingdom. This agreement benefits US Morgan customers by reducing the tariff burden on their dream of Morgan ownership.
Effective June 30, the trade deal lowers the tariff on cars imported into the United States from 27.5% to 10%. This reduced rate applies to up to 100,000 vehicles per year. Morgan understands that any vehicles imported beyond this limit, starting with the 100,001st, will be subject once again to the 27.5% rate.
