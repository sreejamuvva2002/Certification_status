Kia slashes U.S. incentives, reroutes inventory to dodge tariffs

Kia is revamping its U.S. sales strategy to reduce the impact of 25% tariffs on imported vehicles. Instead of raising sticker prices, the company will cut retail incentives—saving an estimated $435 million—and shift Korean-built vehicles to other markets like Canada. U.S. dealers will rely more heavily on inventory from Kia’s Georgia plant going forward.
