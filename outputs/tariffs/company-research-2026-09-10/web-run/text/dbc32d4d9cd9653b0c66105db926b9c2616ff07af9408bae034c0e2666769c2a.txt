[...] ### The Direct Impacts of Tariffs on PCBA Prices

When it comes to tariffs, bare PCBs are classified differently than printed circuit board assemblies. Because of this, they face different tariff rules. If a tariff exemption on PCBAs expires, companies may suddenly have to pay the full tariff rate again. Making sure PCBAs are classified correctly and the country of origin is properly documented can make a big difference in final costs for your company.
