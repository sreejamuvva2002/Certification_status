# 5. Existing commercial technologies

Independently verifiable facts (EPA registration documents, patent numbers,
product labels) are separated below from **company marketing claims**, which are
labelled as such throughout and are *not* treated as evidence that a performance
claim is true.

## 5.1 Verified regulatory anchor

1-MCP is a **registered pesticide active in the United States**, not an
unregulated packaging additive. This is the single most consequential fact for
the project.

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| 1-MCP registered with EPA's Biopesticides and Pollution Prevention Division under the trade name **SmartFresh**, **17 July 2002**, **EPA Reg. No. 71297-2** | USDA AMS 1-MCP petition; EPA pesticide product label `071297-00002` |
| SmartFresh label amended as recently as **2023** (`071297-00002-20230419`) | EPA PPLS |
| **SmartFresh InBox**: powder in a sachet, **EPA Reg. No. 71297-5**, sachet size **1.25 g**, 1 000 sachets per package | AgroFresh product label |
| SmartFresh InBox label cites **US Patents 5,518,988 and 6,017,849** | AgroFresh product label |
| In California, SmartFresh InBox is **approved for kiwifruit only** | AgroFresh CA label |

The last row is the important one for planning. Registration is **crop-specific
and format-specific**. A new delivery format is not covered by an existing
registration simply because the active is the same.

## 5.3 The two findings that matter most

**1. The coated-insert format is already commercial.** HarvestHold Fresh is
described in its own materials and in trade press as a **sheet placed in the box
as produce is packed**. That is, in commercial substance, the architecture
Section 9 recommends. The recommendation remains correct as *engineering* — it is
the cheapest, most scalable, most regulatorily tractable format, and that is
precisely why an incumbent already sells it — but it must not be presented as a
novel format.

**2. Delayed and gradual release is already claimed.** Fresh Inset states that
Vidre+ is *"the first (and only) product in the world that releases 1-MCP with a
delay and gradually, without the need to apply it in a tightly closed"* space
[**company claim, unverified**], describing a *"gradual & timed release
mechanism"* [**company claim**]. Whatever the merits of the superlative, the
*concept* of delayed, gradual, in-package 1-MCP release is commercially occupied
and, per the company, patented.

Taken with the patent landscape (Section 6) and Cao et al. (2024) (Section 2),
this closes off several of the differentiators the brief nominated as possible
white space:

| Candidate differentiator | Status after commercial review |
|---|---|
| Paper/sheet insert format | **Occupied** — HarvestHold Fresh |
| Label / sticker format | **Occupied** — Vidre+ |
| Sachet format | **Occupied** — SmartFresh InBox, Hazel 100 |
| Delayed-onset release | **Occupied (claimed)** — Vidre+ |
| Cyclodextrin carrier | **Occupied** — SmartFresh, AnsiP-G, EasyFresh all cyclodextrin-based |
| Humidity/water activation | **Occupied** — essentially every product above |
| Dual 1-MCP + essential oil on coated paper | **Published** — Cao et al. (2024) |

## 5.4 What is *not* evident in the commercial literature

Across all of the above, the public materials describe **what the products do**,
not **how reproducibly they dose a package**. Specifically, no publicly available
source retrieved here provides, for any commercial product:

* a headspace 1-MCP concentration–time curve in µL·L⁻¹;
* a stated humidity activation threshold;
* a fitted release model or lag time;
* a dosing rule relating insert area or active mass to **package volume and fruit
  mass**, with a stated reproducibility.

This is unsurprising — it is commercially sensitive and not required for a label
— but it defines where a credible technical contribution could still sit. It is
also a *weaker* white space than a format or mechanism gap: absence from public
marketing material is not absence from the companies' internal knowledge or from
their patents.

## 5.5 Cost anchor

The InBox label gives a concrete unit: a **1.25 g sachet**, sold **1 000 per
package**. Any new insert must be costed against that unit — a per-box consumable
at commodity scale, from a supplier with a two-decade-old registration and an
established distribution channel.

Published pricing was not retrievable for any of these products, so the
techno-economic model (Section 15) is built on transparent assumptions with the
cost drivers exposed, rather than on a claimed benchmark price. **Any statement
of competitor price would be a fabrication and none is made.**

## 5.6 Implication for the project

The realistic positioning is **not** "a new format" and **not** "delayed release".
Both are taken. What remains defensible is narrower and quantitative: a
**specified, measured dose-control relationship** — a defined coat weight and
activation threshold delivering a reproducible headspace concentration across a
stated range of package volumes and fruit masses — supported by GC data and a
fitted model that no public source currently provides.

If that cannot be demonstrated to be both real and better than the incumbents,
the honest conclusion is that this project should not proceed as a product play.
Section 17 states the kill criteria in those terms.
